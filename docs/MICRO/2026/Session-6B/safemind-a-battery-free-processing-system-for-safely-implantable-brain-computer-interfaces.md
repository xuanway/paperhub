# SafeMind: A Battery-Free Processing System for Safely Implantable Brain-Computer Interfaces

**中文标题：** SafeMind：面向安全可植入脑机接口的无电池处理系统

**所属 Session：** [Session 6B](index.md) · [MICRO 2026](../index.md)

**作者：** Daye Jung, Yeongwoo Jang, Seunghyun Song, Jangwoo Kim

**来源：** MICRO 2026，pp. 2032–2050；[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/MICRO67795.2026.00142)

## 英文摘要

Brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) are emerging as a transformative technology to treat neurological disorders by establishing direct communication between the brain and external devices. To ensure continuous operation, prior work has primarily focused on battery-powered architectures. However, batteries introduce significant safety risks that fundamentally restrict chronic implantation. While recent efforts explore battery-free alternatives, their limited processing capabilities remain a critical barrier. Thus, existing BCIs fail to simultaneously satisfy two essential requirements for medical applications: stringent safety and high quality of service (QoS). We present SafeMind, the first processor-integrated, battery-free BCI system that enables safe implantation with high-performance processing. We first develop a rigorous cross-layer model incorporating in-vivo brain motion data, coil misalignment, and energy storage dynamics. Leveraging this model, we systematically identify performance challenges of battery-free operation in realistic environments. We then holistically optimize power, communication, and computation through architectural and system-level techniques, designing an end-to-end system that guarantees consistent on-implant processing despite intermittent power. Evaluation shows that SafeMind achieves QoS comparable to battery-powered systems, establishing a practical foundation for safe and reliable next-generation implantable BCIs and future clinical deployment.

## 中文摘要

脑机接口（BCI）正在成为一种变革性技术，通过在大脑和外部设备之间建立直接通信来治疗神经系统疾病。为了确保连续运行，之前的工作主要集中在电池供电的架构上。然而，电池带来了重大的安全风险，从根本上限制了长期植入。尽管最近努力探索无电池替代方案，但其有限的处理能力仍然是一个关键障碍。因此，现有的脑机接口无法同时满足医疗应用的两个基本要求：严格的安全性和高质量的服务（QoS）。我们推出 SafeMind，这是首款集成处理器的无电池 BCI 系统，可通过高性能处理实现安全植入。我们首先开发了一个严格的跨层模型，结合了体内大脑运动数据、线圈错位和能量存储动力学。利用该模型，我们系统地识别了现实环境中无电池运行的性能挑战。然后，我们通过架构和系统级技术全面优化电源、通信和计算，设计一个端到端系统，即使电源间歇性，也能保证一致的植入处理。评估表明，SafeMind 实现了与电池供电系统相当的 QoS，为安全可靠的下一代植入式 BCI 和未来的临床部署奠定了实用基础。
