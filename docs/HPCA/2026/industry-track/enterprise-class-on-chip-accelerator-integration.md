## 中文标题

企业级片上加速器集成

## 英文摘要

The IBM Z® platform and the underlying processor chip designs supporting it are optimized for processing vast amounts of data and transactions, while delivering consistent system performance, throughput, and response latencies with a sustained processor utilization of over 90% under all workload conditions in a highly virtualized and secured computing environment. The IBM Telum® series of processor chip designs that support the platform introduced the industry to a novel modular scalable heterogeneous processor compute framework with an integrated multi-tier unified cache hierarchy all within one chip. The processor chip design leverages a unique approach to ensure all elements work in unison to continuously deliver performance to the evolving needs of mission critical workloads running on the platform while responding to those changing demands at processor clock speeds. This paper will detail the varying compute, accelerator, and cache units within the IBM Telum II processor chip design, how they adaptively work in unison without generating a cacophony of agents competing for scarce hardware resources, and how this forms the backbone of the scalable multi-processor system that our modern economy is built upon.

## 中文摘要

IBM Z® 平台及其背后的处理器芯片设计针对海量数据与事务处理进行了优化，同时在高虚拟化、高安全的计算环境中，于所有负载条件下保持超过 90% 的持续处理器利用率，提供一致的系统性能、吞吐与时延。支撑该平台的 IBM Telum® 系列处理器芯片设计首次为业界引入了新颖的模块化可扩展异构处理器计算框架，以及集成在单芯片内的多层级统一缓存层次。该处理器芯片设计采用独特方法，确保所有部件协同一致地工作，持续为平台上运行的关键任务负载不断演进的需求提供性能，并以处理器时钟速度响应这些变化的需求。本文将详述 IBM Telum II 处理器芯片设计中各类计算、加速器与缓存单元，说明它们如何自适应地协同工作而不产生众多代理争抢稀缺硬件资源的“不和谐”，以及这如何构成支撑现代经济的可扩展多处理器系统的骨干。
