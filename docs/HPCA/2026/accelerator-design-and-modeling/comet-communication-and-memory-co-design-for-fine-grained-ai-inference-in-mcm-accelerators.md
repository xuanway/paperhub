## 中文标题

COMET：面向 MCM 加速器中细粒度 AI 推理的通信与内存协同设计

## 英文摘要

Chiplet-based architectures have emerged as a promising approach to overcome the physical and manufacturing constraints faced by monolithic chips, enabling scalable integration of compute resources to meet the growing demands of AI workloads. However, efficient inter-chiplet communication remains some significant bottleneck, especially under the fine-grained and bursty DMA request patterns generated by processing elements in modern AI tasks. Existing communication models and simulators fail to capture these characteristics, which limits the accuracy of performance analysis and the effectiveness of optimization strategies. These limitations hinder the DMA-communication inefficiencies in chiplet-based AI systems and pose challenges for designing HPC architectures. To address these challenges, we present the first comprehensive chiplet communication model that explicitly incorporates fine-grained DMA traffic observed in realistic AI workloads. Building on this model, we propose COMET, a novel framework that intelligently searches for optimal DMA request aggregation and memory address mapping strategies tailored to chiplet environments. COMET dynamically consolidates small DMA transfers to improve bandwidth utilization and reduce communication latency, while also adapting on-chip memory mapping to align with workload-specific dataflows. This mitigates synchronization overhead across diverse AI tasks. Compared with inference on conventional chiplet communication schemes, COMET achieves 1.7×–2.5× speedup and 1.5×–4.4× higher bandwidth utilization across different DNN and LLM workloads.

## 中文摘要

基于芯粒的架构已成为突破单片芯片物理与制造约束的有效途径，可规模化集成计算资源以满足不断增长的 AI 负载需求。然而，高效的芯粒间通信仍是显著瓶颈，尤其在现代 AI 任务中处理单元产生的细粒度、突发式 DMA 请求模式下。现有通信模型与模拟器无法刻画这些特征，限制了性能分析的准确性与优化策略的有效性，也掩盖了芯粒式 AI 系统中的 DMA 通信低效问题，给 HPC 架构设计带来挑战。为应对这些挑战，我们提出首个显式纳入真实 AI 负载中细粒度 DMA 流量的芯粒通信模型。基于该模型，我们提出 COMET 框架，可智能搜索适合芯粒环境的最优 DMA 请求聚合与内存地址映射策略。COMET 动态合并小规模 DMA 传输以提升带宽利用率、降低通信时延，同时使片上内存映射适配负载特有的数据流，从而缓解各类 AI 任务中的同步开销。与常规芯粒通信方案上的推理相比，COMET 在不同 DNN 与 LLM 负载上取得 1.7 倍至 2.5 倍加速比，带宽利用率提升 1.5 倍至 4.4 倍。
