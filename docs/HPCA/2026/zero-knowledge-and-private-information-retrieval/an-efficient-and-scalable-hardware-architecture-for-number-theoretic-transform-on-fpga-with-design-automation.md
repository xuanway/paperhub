## 中文标题

一种面向 FPGA 的高效可扩展数论变换硬件架构与设计自动化

## 英文摘要

Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) has become a promising approach to protecting data privacy in emerging application scenarios. Unfortunately, FHE suffers from significant processing speed degradation compared to plaintext computation, with one of the primary bottlenecks being the time-consuming Number Theoretic Transform (NTT). Therefore, accelerating NTT to accommodate various FHE parameters is crucial to advancing FHE towards practical use. With highly reconfigurable and performant logical fabrics, Field Programmable Gate Arrays (FPGAs) have exhibited great potential in NTT acceleration. By decomposing large-point NTT with strong data dependency into independent and simple small-point NTTs, the emerging Ten-step NTT (TNTT) algorithms intuitively enable higher parallelism and thereby have the potential to explore better performance compared to traditional algorithms. However, our quantitative analysis reveals that TNTT exhibits significant performance degradation as parallelism increases due to additional varying-size transpositions and Hadamard products. This paper proposes AutoNest, an efficient and scalable hardware architecture, along with an accelerator auto-generation framework for TNTT. The proposed hardware architecture maximizes performance by 1) adopting a 2D block decomposition dataflow to address critical path delays in transpose logic, thereby improving clock frequency. 2) integrating algorithm-level cost-free twiddle factor fusion to reduce the number of modular multiplications in Hadamard products, thereby allowing higher parallelism on chip. Moreover, we also deliver an accelerator generation framework conducting automated design space exploration to elaborate a performant TNTT architecture under the target FPGAs’ resource budget for user-defined FHE parameters. Experimental results on the AMD-Xilinx U280 FPGA demonstrate that NTT accelerators generated by AutoNest achieve an average speedup of 2.31× compared to prior designs.

## 中文摘要

全同态加密（FHE）已成为在新兴应用场景中保护数据隐私的有前景方法。遗憾的是，与明文计算相比 FHE 处理速度显著下降，其主要瓶颈之一是耗时的数论变换（NTT）；因此加速 NTT 以适配多种 FHE 参数，对推动 FHE 实用化至关重要。凭借高度可重构且高性能的逻辑结构，现场可编程门阵列（FPGA）在 NTT 加速上展现出巨大潜力。新兴的十步 NTT（TNTT）算法将具有强数据依赖的大点数 NTT 分解为独立简单的小点数 NTT，直观上可实现更高并行度，从而有望优于传统算法。然而我们的定量分析表明，随着并行度提高，额外的变尺寸转置与 Hadamard 积使 TNTT 出现显著性能下降。本文提出 AutoNest，一种高效可扩展的硬件架构，以及面向 TNTT 的加速器自动生成框架。所提架构通过以下方式最大化性能：1）采用二维分块分解数据流，解决转置逻辑中的关键路径延迟以提升时钟频率；2）集成算法级零开销旋转因子融合，减少 Hadamard 积中的模乘次数以支持片上更高并行度。此外，我们还提供加速器生成框架，自动开展设计空间探索，在目标 FPGA 的资源预算下针对用户定义的 FHE 参数生成高性能 TNTT 架构。在 AMD-Xilinx U280 FPGA 上的结果表明，AutoNest 生成的 NTT 加速器相比先前设计平均取得 2.31 倍加速比。
