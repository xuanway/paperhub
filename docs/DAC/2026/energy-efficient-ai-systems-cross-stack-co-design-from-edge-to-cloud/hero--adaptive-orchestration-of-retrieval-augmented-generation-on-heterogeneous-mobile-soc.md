## 中文标题

Hero：异构移动 SoC 上检索增强生成的自适应编排

## 英文摘要

Agentic retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) is promising for on-device deployment on mobile SoCs, but its multi-stage and dynamic workflow is difficult to schedule efficiently across heterogeneous processors due to accelerator affinity, shape sensitivity, and shared-memory bandwidth contention. We present HeRo, a heterogeneous-aware framework for low-latency on-device agentic RAG. It combines profiling-based performance modeling with a lightweight online scheduler to enable shape-aware partitioning, criticality-aware mapping, and bandwidth-aware concurrency control. Experiments on commercial mobile devices show up to 10.94× lower end-to-end latency over existing strategies, making practical on-device agentic RAG possible.

## 中文摘要

智能体式检索增强生成（RAG）在移动 SoC 上进行端侧部署颇具前景，但由于加速器亲和性、形状敏感性以及共享内存带宽争用，其多阶段、动态的工作流难以在异构处理器之间高效调度。我们提出 HeRo，一个面向低延迟端侧智能体式 RAG 的异构感知框架。它将基于性能剖析的性能建模与轻量级在线调度器相结合，从而实现形状感知的任务划分、关键性感知的映射以及带宽感知的并发控制。在商用移动设备上的实验表明，相比现有策略，端到端延迟最多降低 10.94 倍，使实用的端侧智能体式 RAG 成为可能。
