## 中文标题

PCIe 之魅影：约束生成式人工智能以实现实用的外设迹线合成

## 英文摘要

Peripheral Component Interconnect Express (PCIe) is the de facto interconnect standard for high-speed peripherals and CPUs. The development of PCIe devices for emerging applications requires realistic Transaction Layer Packet (TLP) traces that accurately simulate device-CPU interactions. While generative AI offers a promising avenue for synthesizing complex TLP sequences, it is prone to a critical challenge inherent in all generation tasks: hallucination. Naively applying these models often produces traces that violate fundamental PCIe protocol rules, such as ordering and causality, rendering them unusable for device simulation. To resolve this, our work introduces a methodology to bridge the gap between generative AI and high-fidelity device simulation. This paper presents Phantom, a framework that systematically addresses AI-generated hallucinations in TLP synthesis. Phantom achieves this by coupling a generative backbone with a novel post-processing filter that enforces PCIe-specific constraints, effectively eliminating invalid TLP sequences. We validate Phantom’s effectiveness by synthesizing TLP traces for an actual PCIe network interface card. Experimental results show that Phantom produces practical, large-scale TLP traces, significantly outperforming existing models, with improvements of up to 1000× in task-specific metrics and up to 2.19× in Fréchet Inception Distance (FID) compared to backbone-only methods. The prototype implementation has been made open-source.

## 中文摘要

PCIe（Peripheral Component Interconnect Express）是高速外设与 CPU 之间事实上的互连标准。面向新兴应用开发 PCIe 设备，需要能够准确模拟设备与 CPU 交互的真实事务层数据包（TLP）迹线。尽管生成式 AI 为合成复杂的 TLP 序列提供了有前景的途径，但它也面临所有生成任务固有的一个关键挑战：幻觉。朴素地套用这些模型往往会产生违反 PCIe 基本协议规则（如顺序性与因果性）的迹线，使其无法用于设备仿真。为解决这一问题，我们的工作提出一种方法，弥合生成式 AI 与高保真设备仿真之间的差距。本文介绍 Phantom，一个系统性地应对 TLP 合成中 AI 生成幻觉的框架。Phantom 通过将生成式主干模型与一种新颖的后处理过滤器相耦合来强制执行 PCIe 特有的约束，从而有效消除无效的 TLP 序列。我们通过为一块真实的 PCIe 网络接口卡合成 TLP 迹线来验证 Phantom 的有效性。实验结果表明，Phantom 能够生成实用的大规模 TLP 迹线，显著优于现有模型：相比仅使用主干模型的方法，任务特定指标最高提升 1000 倍，Fréchet Inception Distance（FID）最高提升 2.19 倍。原型实现已开源。
