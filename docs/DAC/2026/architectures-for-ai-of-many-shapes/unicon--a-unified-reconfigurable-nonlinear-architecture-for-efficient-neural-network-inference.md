UNICON: A Unified Reconfigurable Nonlinear Architecture for Efficient Neural Network Inference

## 中文标题

UNICON：面向高效神经网络推理的统一可重构非线性架构

## 英文摘要

Nonlinear activation functions (NAFs) are critical to deep neural networks (DNNs), yet their diverse and complex computational forms are inherently hardware-unfriendly, incurring substantial latency, area, and energy overheads. This work presents UNICON, a unified reconfigurable hardware architecture that efficiently supports diverse NAFs through a logarithmic-domain computing paradigm. UNICON uncovers the intrinsic correlations among NAFs and decomposes complex nonlinear operations into lightweight shift–add operations. With modular design and dynamic reconfigurable dataflows, UNICON achieves high functional flexibility and resource efficiency without hardware duplication. As the first algorithm–architecture co-designed solution that unifies diverse NAFs within a logarithmic-domain framework, UNICON gains an average 1.55× speedup, 2.56× energy efficiency, and 2.74× area efficiency over state-of-the-art NAF architecture.

## 中文摘要

非线性激活函数（NAF）对深度神经网络（DNN）至关重要，然而其多样而复杂的计算形式本质上对硬件不友好，带来可观的延迟、面积与能耗开销。本工作提出 UNICON，一种统一可重构的硬件架构，通过对数域计算范式高效支持多种 NAF。UNICON 揭示了各 NAF 之间的内在关联，并将复杂的非线性运算分解为轻量级的移位—加法操作。借助模块化设计与动态可重构数据流，UNICON 无需硬件复制即可实现高度的功能灵活性与资源效率。作为首个在对数域框架内统一多种 NAF 的算法—架构协同设计解决方案，UNICON 相较于最先进的 NAF 架构平均取得 1.55× 的加速比、2.56× 的能效提升与 2.74× 的面积效率提升。
