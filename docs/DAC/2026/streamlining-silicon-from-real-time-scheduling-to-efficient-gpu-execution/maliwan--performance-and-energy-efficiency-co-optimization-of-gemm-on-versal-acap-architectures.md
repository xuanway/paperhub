MALIWAN: Performance and Energy Efficiency Co-Optimization of GEMM on Versal ACAP Architectures
Authors：
[Ilias Papalamprou](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=4856685001251859673)，[Dimosthenis Masouros](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=920843)，[Ioannis Loudaros](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=65138771724479508241)，[Francky Catthoor](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=08933251173624129561)，[Dimitrios Soudris](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=944143)

## 中文标题

MALIWAN：Versal ACAP 架构上 GEMM 的性能与能效协同优化

## 英文摘要

We introduce MALIWAN, an automated framework that generates Pareto-optimal GEMM mappings on Versal ACAP devices. MALIWAN combines fast analytical-model–based sampling with data-driven ML, using on-board measurements to train a surrogate that drives large-scale Design Space Exploration. Based on a collection of ≈6,000 on-board experiments, we first provide a comprehensive analysis of how different mapping configurations affect performance and power. We then evaluate MALIWAN on the Versal VCK190, demonstrating geomean improvements of 1.23× (up to 2.5×) in throughput and 1.25× (up to 2.7×) in energy efficiency over state-of-the-art works. Compared to NVIDIA GPUs, MALIWAN achieves up to 2.2× energy efficiency.

## 中文摘要

我们提出 MALIWAN，一个可在 Versal ACAP 器件上生成 Pareto 最优 GEMM 映射的自动化框架。MALIWAN 将基于快速解析模型的采样与数据驱动的机器学习相结合，利用片上实测数据训练代理模型，以驱动大规模设计空间探索。基于约 6000 次片上实验，我们首先全面分析了不同映射配置对性能和功耗的影响。随后我们在 Versal VCK190 上评估 MALIWAN，相较于现有最优工作，其吞吐率取得 1.23×（最高 2.5×）的几何平均提升，能效取得 1.25×（最高 2.7×）的提升。与 NVIDIA GPU 相比，MALIWAN 的能效最高可达 2.2×。
