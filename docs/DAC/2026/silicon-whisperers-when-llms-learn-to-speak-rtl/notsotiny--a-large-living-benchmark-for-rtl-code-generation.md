## 中文标题

NotSoTiny：一个面向 RTL 代码生成的大型动态基准

## 英文摘要

LLMs have shown early promise in generating RTL code, yet evaluating their capabilities in realistic settings remains challenging. So far, RTL benchmarks have been limited in scale, skewed toward trivial designs, offering minimal verification rigor, and remaining vulnerable to data contamination. To overcome these limitations and push the field forward, this paper introduces NotSoTiny, a benchmark that assesses LLMs on generating structurally rich and context-aware RTL. Built from hundreds of actual hardware designs produced by the Tiny Tapeout community, our automated pipeline removes duplicates, verifies correctness and periodically incorporates new designs to mitigate contamination, matching Tiny Tapeout release schedule. Evaluation results show that NotSoTiny is more challenging than prior benchmarks and highlight its effectiveness in guiding improvements of LLM-driven hardware design.

## 中文摘要

LLM 在生成 RTL 代码方面已展现出初步潜力，但在真实场景下评估其能力仍然具有挑战性。迄今为止，RTL 基准在规模上受限、偏向简单设计、验证严谨性不足，且仍易受数据污染影响。为克服这些局限并推动该领域发展，本文提出 NotSoTiny，一个用于评估 LLM 生成结构丰富且具备上下文感知能力的 RTL 的基准。该基准基于 Tiny Tapeout 社区产出的数百个实际硬件设计构建，我们的自动化流水线会去除重复项、验证正确性，并与 Tiny Tapeout 的发布节奏同步定期纳入新设计，以缓解数据污染。评估结果表明，NotSoTiny 比以往的基准更具挑战性，并凸显了其在指导 LLM 驱动的硬件设计改进方面的有效性。
