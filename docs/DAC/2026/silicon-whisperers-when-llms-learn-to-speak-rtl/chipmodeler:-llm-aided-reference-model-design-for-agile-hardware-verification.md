## 中文标题

ChipModeler：面向敏捷硬件验证的 LLM 辅助参考模型设计

## 英文摘要

As the complexity of integrated circuit designs continues to escalate, the functional verification becomes increasingly challenging. Reference models, critical for accelerating the verification process, are themselves becoming more intricate and time-consuming to develop. Despite the promise shown by large language models (LLMs) in code programming, effectively generating complex reference models remains a significant hurdle. Therefore, we introduce ChipModeler, an LLM-aided agile reference model generation and verification platform. ChipModeler streamlines the transition from design specifications to fully functional reference models by integrating design standardization and hierarchical agile modeling. Employing a building-block generation strategy, it not only enhances the design capabilities of LLMs for reference models but also significantly boosts verification efficiency. We evaluated ChipModeler on 300 designs of varying complexity, demonstrating substantial improvements in both efficiency and quality of reference model generation. ChipModeler achieved a peak performance improvement of 58.99% compared to alternative methods, with notable enhancements in generation stability, and delivered a 9.18× increase in its capacity to produce reference model designs. Moreover, ChipModeler accelerates the reference model design and validation cycles by an average of 7.11× over traditional manual approaches. These results highlight the potential of ChipModeler to significantly advance the automation of reference model generation and validation.

## 中文摘要

随着集成电路设计复杂度持续攀升，功能验证变得日益困难。参考模型对加速验证流程至关重要，但其自身的开发也正变得愈加复杂且耗时。尽管大语言模型（LLM）在代码编程方面展现出潜力，有效生成复杂参考模型仍是一大障碍。因此，我们提出 ChipModeler，一个 LLM 辅助的敏捷参考模型生成与验证平台。ChipModeler 通过融合设计标准化与层次化敏捷建模，简化了从设计规格到功能完备参考模型的转化过程。它采用积木式生成策略，不仅增强了 LLM 对参考模型的设计能力，还显著提升了验证效率。我们在 300 个复杂度各异的设计上评估了 ChipModeler，结果显示参考模型生成的效率与质量均有大幅提升。与其他方法相比，ChipModeler 的峰值性能提升达 58.99%，生成稳定性也显著增强，其产出参考模型设计的能力提升 9.18 倍。此外，相较于传统人工方法，ChipModeler 将参考模型的设计与验证周期平均加速 7.11 倍。这些结果凸显了 ChipModeler 在显著推进参考模型生成与验证自动化方面的潜力。
