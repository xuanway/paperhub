## 中文标题

COmPOSER：面向高效实现的毫米波/射频电路性能导向综合与优化

## 英文摘要

This work presents COmPOSER, an open-source, end-to-end framework for RF/mm-wave design automation that translates target specifications into optimized circuits with layouts. It unifies schematic synthesis, layout generation for actives/passives, and place-and-route, incorporating physics-based equations and machinelearning-driven electromagnetic models. Based on post-layout validation on multiple LNAs and PAs at up to 60 GHz in a commercial 65 nm process, COmPOSER meets performance targets, comparable to manual designs, and delivers a 100–300× productivity gain.

## 中文摘要

本文提出 COmPOSER，一个面向射频/毫米波设计自动化的开源端到端框架，可将目标指标转化为带版图的优化电路。它统一了原理图综合、有源/无源器件版图生成以及布局布线，并融合了基于物理的方程与机器学习驱动的电磁模型。基于在商用 65 nm 工艺中最高 60 GHz 的多个 LNA 和 PA 上进行的后版图验证，COmPOSER 达到了与人工设计相当的性能目标，并带来 100–300 倍的生产率提升。
