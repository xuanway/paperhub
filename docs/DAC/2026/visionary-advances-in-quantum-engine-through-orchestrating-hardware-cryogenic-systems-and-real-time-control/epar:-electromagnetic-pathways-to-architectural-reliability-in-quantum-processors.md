EPAR: Electromagnetic Pathways to Architectural Reliability in Quantum Processors
Authors：
[Navnil Choudhury](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=2762082524794180754)，[Yizhuo Tan](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=8443366919637691093)，[Jiaqi Yu](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=61642656904569788571)，[Jakub Szefer](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=0580596946422443058)，[Kanad Basu](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=69760886002233208821)

## 中文标题

EPAR：通往量子处理器架构可靠性的电磁路径

## 英文摘要

As superconducting processors scale, understanding how physical layout shapes qubit interactions is essential for architectural reliability. Existing methods offer limited insight into how electromagnetic design choices translate into execution-level behavior. We present EPAR, an electromagnetic-to-architecture framework that predicts robustness early directly from physical design by reconstructing how design distortion modifies the effective Hamiltonian, reroutes mediated connectivity, and influences control-pulse response. Across all tested layouts, EPAR’s structural scores show 100% agreement with two-qubit error trends yet reveal over 10× robustness differences among edges with identical calibrated error rates, going beyond conventional metrics to provide improved and actionable compiler guidance.

## 中文摘要

随着超导处理器规模不断扩大，理解物理布局如何塑造量子比特之间的相互作用，对架构可靠性至关重要。现有方法难以揭示电磁设计选择如何转化为执行层面的行为。我们提出 EPAR，一个从电磁到架构的框架，可直接依据物理设计在早期预测鲁棒性：它重建设计畸变如何改变有效哈密顿量、重新路由中介连接并影响控制脉冲响应。在所有测试布局中，EPAR 的结构性评分与两比特错误趋势 100% 一致，同时揭示出在校准错误率相同的边上存在超过 10 倍的鲁棒性差异，从而超越传统指标，为编译器提供更优且可操作的指导。
