## Session Description

This session highlights the growing maturity of Compute in and Near Memory, addressing a wide range of applications and technologies. This includes solving differential equations, graph pattern mining, scientific computing and genome analysis workflows. Of course, AI cannot be forgotten and is there too. Papers in this session leverage a wide range of technologies from foundry SRAM of today and stacked flash solutions to up-and-coming RRAM and memristive technologies. This session highlights the breadth of areas that memory-centric computing will impact and seeks an answer to the often repeated question of "are we there yet?"
