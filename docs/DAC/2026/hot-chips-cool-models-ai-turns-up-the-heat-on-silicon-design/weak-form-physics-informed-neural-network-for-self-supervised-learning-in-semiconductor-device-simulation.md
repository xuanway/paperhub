Weak-Form Physics-Informed Neural Network for Self-Supervised Learning in Semiconductor Device Simulation
Authors：

## 中文标题

面向半导体器件仿真的弱形式物理信息神经网络自监督学习方法

## 英文摘要

Technology Computer-Aided Design (TCAD) simulation of semiconductor devices is time-consuming. Existing machine learning surrogate models offer acceleration but require extensive labeled data from these slow TCAD simulations. To address this data dependency issue, we propose the Weak-Form Physics-Informed Neural Network (WF-PINN), a self-supervised method driven solely by physical laws and Dirichlet boundary conditions. WF-PINN utilizes an integral-based weak-form loss to eliminate the need for internal labeled solution data. Experimental results show that WF-PINN effectively simulates the physical characteristics of Fin Field-Effect Transistors, with all solution errors less than 5.36 × 10−2. Furthermore, WF-PINN’s inference speed is 2.16 × 104 times that of TCAD simulation.

## 中文摘要

半导体器件的技术计算机辅助设计（TCAD）仿真耗时较长。现有的机器学习代理模型虽能实现加速，但需要从这些缓慢的 TCAD 仿真中获取大量带标签数据。为解决这一数据依赖问题，我们提出弱形式物理信息神经网络（WF-PINN），一种仅由物理规律与 Dirichlet 边界条件驱动的自监督方法。WF-PINN 采用基于积分的弱形式损失，从而不再需要求解域内部的标签数据。实验结果表明，WF-PINN 能够有效模拟鳍式场效应晶体管（FinFET）的物理特性，所有解的误差均小于 5.36 × 10⁻²。此外，WF-PINN 的推理速度是 TCAD 仿真的 2.16 × 10⁴ 倍。
