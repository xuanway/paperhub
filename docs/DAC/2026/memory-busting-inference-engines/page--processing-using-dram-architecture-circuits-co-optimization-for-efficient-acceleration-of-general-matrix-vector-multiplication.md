## 中文标题

PAGE：面向通用矩阵向量乘法高效加速的 Processing-Using-DRAM 架构与电路协同优化

## 英文摘要

As large language models (LLMs) scale in size/complexity, DRAM access has become a performance bottleneck, especially during the memory-bound decoding phase dominated by general matrix-vector multiplication (GEMV) operations. Among near-/in-memory computing works that address such bottlenecks, Processing-Using-DRAM (PUD) offers a unique advantage as it performs logic operations entirely within DRAM peripherals with minimal circuit changes, enabling deployment on commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) DRAM. While prior work reported multi-input logic operations, accelerating GEMV operations in LLMs remains underexplored due to the limited compute capability of DRAM sense amplifiers (SAs). To overcome this, we propose PAGE, a PUD architecture that accelerates GEMV with four key optimizations: (1) mat-level parallelism that activates multiple consecutive mats; (2) inversion-enabled SA supporting NOT operations within a mat; (3) AP-based row copy mechanism that performs source–destination co–activation with only control-signal changes; and (4) adaptive adder-tree accumulation that reduces accumulation cycles. We demonstrate the baseline PUD architecture on a COTS DRAM–FPGA platform to verify the functionality/timing of PUD operations, implement the modified circuits in TSMC 16nm for circuit-level evaluation, and build an in-house simulator for system-level throughput/energy analysis. Overall, PAGE achieves up to 14×/3.1× end-to-end throughput/energy improvement for GEMV workloads over SoTA PUD designs, demonstrating efficient acceleration of memory-bound LLM workloads.

## 中文摘要

随着大语言模型（LLM）在规模与复杂度上不断攀升，DRAM 访存已成为性能瓶颈，尤其体现在由通用矩阵向量乘法（GEMV）主导、受存储带宽约束的解码阶段。在缓解此类瓶颈的各类近存/存内计算工作中，Processing-Using-DRAM（PUD）具有独特优势：它完全在 DRAM 外围电路内部完成逻辑运算，仅需极小的电路改动，因而可部署于商用现货（COTS）DRAM。尽管已有工作报道了多输入逻辑运算，但受限于 DRAM 灵敏放大器（SA）有限的计算能力，如何在 LLM 中加速 GEMV 运算仍缺乏充分探索。为此，我们提出 PAGE，一种通过四项关键优化加速 GEMV 的 PUD 架构：（1）mat 级并行，可同时激活多个连续的 mat；（2）支持反相的灵敏放大器，可在 mat 内完成 NOT 运算；（3）基于 AP 的行拷贝机制，仅通过改变控制信号即可实现源端与目的端的协同激活；（4）自适应加法树累加，可减少累加周期。我们在 COTS DRAM-FPGA 平台上演示了基线 PUD 架构，以验证 PUD 操作的功能与时序；在 TSMC 16nm 工艺下实现改进电路以开展电路级评估；并构建自研模拟器用于系统级吞吐与能耗分析。总体而言，针对 GEMV 负载，PAGE 相较当前最优（SoTA）PUD 设计可实现最高 14 倍的端到端吞吐提升与 3.1 倍的能耗改善，展现了对受存储带宽约束的 LLM 负载的高效加速能力。
