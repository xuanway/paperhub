3D-ReSAC: A Near-3D-Stacked-DRAM Processor with Area-Efficient Sequential Refresh-Skip and High-Link-Utilization Arrowmesh Communication for Fast Low-Batch LLM Inference
Authors：
[Wenbin Jia](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=3125365595972588827)，[Haocheng Li](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=9214178535727079568)，[Xiang Li](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=44358680740329397771)，[Xinyuan Lin](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=3697456073736168717)，[Yongpan Liu](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=245143)

## 中文标题

3D-ReSAC：面向快速低批次 LLM 推理的近 3D 堆叠 DRAM 处理器，具备面积高效的顺序刷新跳过与高链路利用率 Arrowmesh 通信

## 英文摘要

Low-batch LLM inference on edge hardware places stringent demands on both memory bandwidth and computational capacity. While 3D-stacked DRAM accelerators offer a promising solution, they introduce two critical overheads that are frequently under-optimized: DRAM refresh and collective communication. To mitigate these issues, we propose 3D-ReSAC, a near-memory processor based on 3D-stacked DRAM, equipped with an area-efficient refresh-skip method and high-link-utilization ArrowMesh communication. Our evaluations show that 3D-ReSAC reduces refresh and communication overheads by 7–100% and 53–75%, respectively, leading to a 1.12× to 2.02× latency reduction across low-batch LLM inference workloads.

## 中文摘要

边缘硬件上的低批次 LLM 推理对存储带宽与计算能力均提出严苛要求。3D 堆叠 DRAM 加速器虽提供了颇具前景的解决方案，却引入了两项常被优化不足的关键开销：DRAM 刷新与集合通信。为缓解上述问题，我们提出 3D-ReSAC，一种基于 3D 堆叠 DRAM 的近存处理器，配备面积高效的刷新跳过方法与高链路利用率的 ArrowMesh 通信机制。评估结果表明，3D-ReSAC 将刷新开销与通信开销分别降低 7–100% 与 53–75%，在各类低批次 LLM 推理负载上实现 1.12 倍至 2.02 倍的延迟下降。
