Detrojan: Detecting Trojan Circuits Using a Purpose-Built Graph Neural Network with Community-Enhanced Classification
Authors：
[Chien-Tung (Cherie) Kuo](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=6505428644710341119)，[Chen-Ching Nieh](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=7344555516006120169)，[Jie-Hong Roland Jiang](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=044143)，[Chung-Yang (Ric) Huang](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=691443)

## 中文标题

DeTrojan：基于专用图神经网络与社区增强分类的木马电路检测

## 英文摘要

Gate-level Hardware Trojan (HT) detection using Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) often suffers from limited accuracy due to the reliance on structure-only features and fragmented node-level predictions. We propose a GNN-based framework, DeTrojan, to overcome these challenges through three key innovations: (1) functionality-aware feature engineering, (2) an edge-aware GNN architecture with Jumping Knowledge and global context aggregation, and (3) accurate Trojan circuit localization with community-aware classification refinement. Evaluated on the 2025 ICCAD CAD Contest Hardware Trojan benchmarks, DeTrojan achieves a 16.6% improvement in circuit-level prediction accuracy and a 47.3% relative increase in gate-level F1-score over the state-of-the-art machine learning (ML)- based methods. Furthermore, incorporating our proposed features and refinement modules into the existing approaches yield up to a 10.5% gain in accuracy and a 50.8% increase in the F1-score, demonstrating the effectiveness and generality of our framework. Notably, DeTrojan secured first place in the 2025 ICCAD CAD Contest and will be open-sourced to facilitate further research.

## 中文摘要

基于图神经网络（GNN）的门级硬件木马（HT）检测常因仅依赖结构特征和碎片化的节点级预测而精度受限。我们提出基于 GNN 的框架 DeTrojan，通过三项关键创新克服上述挑战：(1) 功能感知的特征工程；(2) 具备 Jumping Knowledge 与全局上下文聚合的边感知 GNN 架构；(3) 借助社区感知分类精化的精确木马电路定位。在 2025 ICCAD CAD Contest 硬件木马基准上的评估表明，相比最优的基于机器学习（ML）的方法，DeTrojan 在电路级预测准确率上提升 16.6%，在门级 F1 分数上实现 47.3% 的相对提升。此外，将我们提出的特征与精化模块并入现有方法，可带来最高 10.5% 的准确率增益和 50.8% 的 F1 分数提升，证明了本框架的有效性与通用性。值得注意的是，DeTrojan 在 2025 ICCAD CAD Contest 中荣获第一名，并将开源以促进后续研究。
