STAMP: Semiconductor Tool-Chain Attestation Through Wafer-Level Machine Learning-Based Process Monitoring
Authors：
[Suraag Sunil Tellakula](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=2093743492902558833)，[Ching-Yi Chang](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=483743)，[Matthew Nigh](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=1484985089596165682)，[John Carulli](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=53587092449608841111)，[Yiorgos Makris](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=6095933710024952258)

## 中文标题

STAMP：基于晶圆级机器学习工艺监控的半导体工具链证明

## 英文摘要

We introduce a machine learning approach for distinguishing between wafers fabricated using a ratified chain of tools on a semiconductor manufacturing floor and unratified versions of the tool-chain based on metrology or wafer acceptance tests collected during manufacturing and testing. Our method exploits the systematic nature of process variation and captures the subtle causal relationships between tool exchanges and the resulting changes in physical dimensions or electrical characteristics of the silicon, which can then be used for enabling wafer-level tool-chain attestation. Effectiveness of our solution is demonstrated on a dataset of inline and e-test measurements from 7000 wafers fabricated with multiple tool-chain variants in a 12nm FinFET technology node.

## 中文摘要

我们提出一种机器学习方法，根据制造与测试过程中采集的量测数据或晶圆验收测试数据，区分在半导体制造产线上由经核准工具链制造的晶圆与由未经核准工具链版本制造的晶圆。我们的方法利用工艺偏差的系统性本质，捕捉工具更换与硅片物理尺寸或电学特性随之变化之间的细微因果关系，进而支撑晶圆级工具链证明。我们在 12nm FinFET 工艺节点下、采用多种工具链变体制造的 7000 片晶圆的内联测量与电性测试数据集上验证了该方案的有效性。
