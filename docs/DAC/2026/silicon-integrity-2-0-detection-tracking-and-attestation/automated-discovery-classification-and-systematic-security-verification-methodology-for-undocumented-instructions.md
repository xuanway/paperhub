Automated Discovery, Classification, and Systematic Security Verification Methodology for Undocumented Instructions
Authors：

## 中文标题

面向未文档化指令的自动化发现、分类与系统化安全验证方法

## 英文摘要

Undocumented instructions have attracted growing interest and emerged as a critical threat, given their potential to undermine system stability, security, and availability. Considering the expanding attack surface at the processor instruction level, existing security research predominantly concentrates on the initial discovery of undocumented instructions, often relying on constrained fuzzing techniques that inherently limit the effective search space. Furthermore, the crucial step of determining the operational semantics of these newly found instructions remains entirely reliant on labor-intensive, ad-hoc manual analysis. Additionally, there is a significant research gap concerning systematic methodologies for verifying and assessing the security implications and threats associated with newly found undocumented instructions. This paper addresses these critical challenges by presenting a comprehensive methodology for the automated discovery, systematic classification, and security verification of undocumented instructions within commercial RISC processors. The comprehensive evaluation highlights the efficacy of our approach: we discovered 23 unique undocumented instructions and we classified 29 undocumented instructions via classifier that achieved an instruction classification accuracy of 99.8357%. Most importantly, our threat evaluation approach verified 5 instructions exhibiting verifiable security implications.

## 中文摘要

未文档化指令可能破坏系统稳定性、安全性与可用性，其潜在威胁已引起日益广泛的关注，并成为一类关键安全隐患。鉴于处理器指令层面的攻击面不断扩大，现有安全研究主要集中于未文档化指令的初步发现，且多依赖受限的模糊测试技术，这本身即限制了有效的搜索空间。此外，确定新发现指令运行语义这一关键步骤仍完全依赖人力密集的、逐案的手工分析。同时，对于系统化验证与评估新发现未文档化指令的安全影响与威胁的方法论，研究上仍存在明显空白。本文针对上述关键挑战，提出了一套完整方法，用于在商用 RISC 处理器中实现未文档化指令的自动化发现、系统化分类与安全验证。全面评估结果充分体现了该方法的有效性：我们发现了 23 条独特的未文档化指令，并通过分类器对 29 条未文档化指令完成了分类，指令分类准确率达 99.8357%。最重要的是，我们的威胁评估方法验证了 5 条具有可验证安全影响的指令。
