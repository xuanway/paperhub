XRel-Graph: Graph Learning-Driven Cross-Layer Reliability Management in Embedded Mixed-Criticality Systems
Authors：

## 中文标题

XRel-Graph：嵌入式混合关键性系统中图学习驱动的跨层可靠性管理

## 英文摘要

Technology scaling in embedded Mixed-Criticality (MC) systems intensifies reliability challenges, impacting both reliable execution of MC applications and underlying hardware. Ensuring real-time and safety requirements is essential in these MC systems. While some studies have explored applying fault tolerance techniques in MC systems in multiple abstraction layers separately, such isolated layer-specific fault mitigation incurs high overheads in peak power, resources, energy, and timing, making it impractical for most embedded MC systems. In this regard, we propose a novel scheme to address cross-layer reliability in MC systems at design-time, by providing application-specific, low-cost fault tolerance. This approach distributes fault mitigation across multiple layers to minimize it on the hardware layer, ultimately resulting in more cost-effective system designs. To achieve this, we introduce a machine-learning-based approach to select efficient fault-tolerance methods across multiple layers, reducing overheads while meeting the real-time and reliability requirements. Experimental results demonstrate our scheme meets reliability constraints for all task criticality levels, outperforming single-layer method, while reducing peak power and energy consumption by an average of 27.55% and 48.32%, respectively. Compared to state-of-the-art, it achieves 50.54% lower peak power and 76.67% lower energy, while meeting the requirements.

## 中文摘要

嵌入式混合关键性（Mixed-Criticality，MC）系统中的工艺微缩加剧了可靠性挑战，既影响 MC 应用的可靠执行，也影响底层硬件。在这类 MC 系统中，确保实时性与安全性要求至关重要。尽管已有研究分别在多个抽象层上探索了在 MC 系统中应用容错技术，但这种孤立的、针对特定层的故障缓解会在峰值功耗、资源、能量与时序方面带来高开销，对大多数嵌入式 MC 系统而言并不实用。为此，我们提出一种新方案，在设计阶段以应用定制的低成本容错来解决 MC 系统中的跨层可靠性问题。该方法将故障缓解分布到多个层，以最小化硬件层上的开销，最终得到更具成本效益的系统设计。为实现这一点，我们引入基于机器学习的方法，跨多个层选择高效的容错方法，在满足实时性与可靠性要求的同时降低开销。实验结果表明，我们的方案满足所有任务关键性等级的可靠性约束，优于单层方法，同时将峰值功耗与能耗平均分别降低 27.55% 与 48.32%。与最先进工作相比，在满足要求的同时实现了低 50.54% 的峰值功耗与低 76.67% 的能耗。
