RTFL: Energy-Aware Federated Learning for AIoT Design Via Adaptive Quantization-Based Multi-Agent Scheduling
Authors：
[Jun Xia](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=65023396002461221351)，[Junqi Zhang](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=6533152226655894793)，[Zhaorong Zhu](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=4771685053263135444)，[Wenjie Chen](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=710131727574074045)，[Mingsong Chen](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=923143)

## 中文标题

RTFL：基于自适应量化多智能体调度的 AIoT 能量感知联邦学习设计

## 英文摘要

Due to the varying computing and communication capabilities of resource-constrained devices, Federated Learning (FL) suffers from the problems of slow convergence and poor training performance, especially when devices are powered by batteries. This paper introduces RTFL, a novel energy-aware Real-Time Federated Learning framework based on Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL), aiming to enhance knowledge sharing across AIoT devices within a specified training time constraint. Specifically, RTFL uses an Adaptive Quantization-based Multi-Agent Scheduling (AQMAS) strategy, where multiple agents intelligently select devices with appropriate model quantization levels for each local training round, based on the devices’ current resource constraints. By facilitating collaboration among agents through reinforcement learning, RTFL enables devices to maximize their contributions to forming an optimal global model, while balancing the trade-off between the accuracy of quantized models and the limited resources available on each device. Comprehensive experiments show that RTFL accelerates FL convergence and encourages devices to join more aggregation rounds, significantly improving overall training performance.

## 中文摘要

由于资源受限设备的计算与通信能力各不相同，联邦学习（FL）存在收敛缓慢和训练性能差的问题，尤其是当设备由电池供电时。本文提出 RTFL，一种基于多智能体强化学习（MARL）的新型能量感知实时联邦学习框架，旨在给定的训练时间约束内增强 AIoT 设备间的知识共享。具体而言，RTFL 采用基于自适应量化的多智能体调度（AQMAS）策略，其中多个智能体根据设备当前的资源约束，为每个本地训练轮次智能地选择具有合适模型量化级别的设备。通过强化学习促进智能体之间的协作，RTFL 使设备能够最大化其对形成最优全局模型的贡献，同时平衡量化模型的精度与各设备可用有限资源之间的权衡。综合实验表明，RTFL 加速了 FL 收敛，并促使设备参与更多聚合轮次，显著提升了整体训练性能。
