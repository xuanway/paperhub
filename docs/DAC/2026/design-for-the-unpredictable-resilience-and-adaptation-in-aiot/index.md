## Session Description

This session presents recent advances in the design of components and methodologies that enable resilient, reliable, and low-energy systems. The presentations cover error-correction codes for neural networks, the design of safe and reliable NPUs and controllers, run-time analyzers, and innovative techniques for reasoning and adaptation. Together, these talks demonstrate how cross-layer hardware components, design methodologies, and run-time analysis are shaping the next generation of robust and trustworthy autonomous systems.
