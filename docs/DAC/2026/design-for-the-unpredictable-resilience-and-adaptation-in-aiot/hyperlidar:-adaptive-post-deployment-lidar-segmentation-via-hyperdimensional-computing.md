## 中文标题

HyperLiDAR：基于超维计算的自适应部署后 LiDAR 分割

## 英文摘要

LiDAR semantic segmentation plays a pivotal role in 3D scene understanding for edge applications such as autonomous driving. However, significant challenges remain for real-world deployments, particularly for on-device post-deployment adaptation. Real-world environments can shift as the system navigates through different locations, leading to substantial performance degradation without effective and timely model adaptation. Furthermore, edge systems operate under strict computational and energy constraints, making it infeasible to adapt conventional segmentation models (based on large neural networks) directly on-device. To address the above challenges, we introduce HyperLiDAR, the first lightweight, post-deployment LiDAR segmentation framework based on Hyperdimensional Computing (HDC). The design of HyperLiDAR fully leverages the fast learning and high efficiency of HDC, inspired by how the human brain processes information. To further improve the adaptation efficiency, we identify the high data volume per scan as a key bottleneck and introduce a buffer selection strategy that focuses learning on the most informative points. We conduct extensive evaluations on two state-of-the-art LiDAR segmentation benchmarks and two representative devices. Our results show that HyperLiDAR outperforms or achieves comparable adaptation performance to state-of-the-art segmentation methods, while achieving up to a 13.8× speedup in retraining.

## 中文摘要

LiDAR 语义分割在自动驾驶等边缘应用的 3D 场景理解中起着关键作用。然而，真实世界部署仍面临重大挑战，尤其是设备端的部署后自适应。当系统在不同地点之间移动时，真实环境可能发生偏移，若缺乏有效且及时的模型自适应，将导致性能显著下降。此外，边缘系统在严格的计算与能量约束下运行，使得直接在设备端自适应传统的分割模型（基于大型神经网络）变得不可行。为应对上述挑战，我们提出 HyperLiDAR，首个基于超维计算（HDC）的轻量级部署后 LiDAR 分割框架。HyperLiDAR 的设计充分借助 HDC 的快速学习与高效率，其灵感来自人脑处理信息的方式。为进一步提升自适应效率，我们识别出单次扫描的数据量过大是关键瓶颈，并引入一种缓冲区选择策略，将学习集中在最具信息量的点上。我们在两个最先进的 LiDAR 分割基准和两种代表性设备上开展了广泛评估。结果表明，HyperLiDAR 的自适应性能优于或相当于最先进的分割方法，同时重训练最高实现 13.8× 加速。
