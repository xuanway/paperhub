Timing-Driven Scheduling and Placement Under Pressure-Path Interference for Fully Programmable Valve Array Biochips

## 中文标题

压力路径干扰下全可编程阀阵列生物芯片的时序驱动调度与布局

## 英文摘要

Fully Programmable Valve Array (FPVA) biochips have attracted widespread attention due to their flexibility and reusability. Despite these advantages, their practical application is challenged by the inherently time-sensitive nature of many bioassays, which require precise fluidic control within strict time window. Violating these timing constraints can cause deviations or even assay failures. Additionally, the pneumatic actuation used to drive fluids forms pressure paths that cause resource contention, thereby delaying fluid transport. However, existing methods rarely address timing constraints and typically consider pressure-path interference only during routing, overlooking its broader impact on earlier design stages. To address these issues, we propose a timing-driven scheduling and placement framework that explicitly considers pressure-path interference, enabling more efficient and reliable fluid operations. The framework first generates timing-aware phase schedules using a tree-level allocation strategy, and then performs a T-tree structured dynamic particle swarm optimization method to improve routability and shorten transport paths. Experiments on multiple benchmarks demonstrate that our framework significantly enhances both the efficiency and robustness of FPVA biochips.

## 中文摘要

全可编程阀阵列（FPVA）生物芯片因其灵活性和可重用性而受到广泛关注。尽管具备这些优势，许多生物检测固有的时间敏感性仍对其实际应用构成挑战，这些检测要求在严格的时间窗口内实现精确的流体控制。违反这些时序约束可能导致偏差甚至检测失败。此外，用于驱动流体的气动致动会形成压力路径并引发资源争用，从而延迟流体传输。然而，现有方法很少处理时序约束，并且通常仅在布线阶段考虑压力路径干扰，忽视了其对更早设计阶段的更广泛影响。为解决这些问题，我们提出一个显式考虑压力路径干扰的时序驱动调度与布局框架，从而实现更高效、更可靠的流体操作。该框架首先采用树级分配策略生成时序感知的阶段调度，然后执行 T 树结构动态粒子群优化方法以改善可布线性并缩短传输路径。在多个基准上的实验表明，我们的框架显著提升了 FPVA 生物芯片的效率和鲁棒性。
