Crosstalk Timing Prediction via Graph Prompt Learning in Aggressor-Victim RC Networks

## 中文标题

基于图提示学习的攻击者—受害者 RC 网络串扰时序预测

## 英文摘要

As technology nodes continue to scale, crosstalk effects have emerged as a critical challenge in timing closure for modern integrated circuits. Conventional graph learning-based wire timing models fall short in accurately predicting crosstalk timing, due to their limited capability to model intricate coupling behaviors between RC components and complex interactions between aggressor and victim nets. To overcome these limitations, we introduce a graph prompt learning framework specifically designed for crosstalk timing estimation. Our framework incorporates a dual-level prompt architecture that utilizes node-level prompts to capture fine-grained coupling effects and employs subgraph-level prompts to model holistic multi-net interactions, thereby effectively adapting the pre-trained model to crosstalk timing prediction. Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves precise and fast crosstalk timing prediction, and consistently outperforms existing methods in several benchmark designs.

## 中文摘要

随着工艺节点持续微缩，串扰效应已成为现代集成电路时序收敛中的关键挑战。传统基于图学习的线网时序模型难以准确预测串扰时序，原因在于其刻画 RC 元件之间复杂耦合行为以及攻击者与受害者线网之间复杂交互的能力有限。为克服这些局限，我们提出一种专为串扰时序估计设计的图提示学习框架。该框架采用双层提示架构，利用节点级提示捕捉细粒度耦合效应，并采用子图级提示建模整体性的多线网交互，从而有效地将预训练模型适配至串扰时序预测任务。实验结果表明，所提框架能够实现精确且快速的串扰时序预测，并在多个基准设计中持续优于现有方法。
