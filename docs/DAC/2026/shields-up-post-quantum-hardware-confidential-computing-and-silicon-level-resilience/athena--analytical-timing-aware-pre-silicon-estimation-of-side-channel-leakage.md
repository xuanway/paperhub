Athena: Analytical Timing-Aware Pre-Silicon Estimation of Side-Channel Leakage
Authors：
[C.Rohin Menon](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=9708705174792795583)，[Annapurna Valiveti](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=573817808841537718)，[Janakiraman Viraraghavan](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=38251502693200025971)，[Chester Rebeiro](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=950443)

## 中文标题

Athena：面向侧信道泄漏的解析式时序感知流片前评估

## 英文摘要

We present Athena, a scalable analytical framework for pre-silicon side-channel leakage evaluation that avoids simulation entirely. Athena integrates static timing information with signal-probability propagation to model time-dependent circuit behavior, capturing leakage arising from input-arrival skew, glitches, and other timing-induced effects. The fine-grained leakage estimates produced precisely identify vulnerable signals and time intervals, and support evaluation of diverse countermeasures including masking, gate resizing, and differential logic. We evaluate Athena across a broad set of countermeasures on S-Boxes and full ciphers, comparing both correctness and performance against state-of-the-art tools. Across all benchmarks, Athena delivers over 400× speedup over existing methods.

## 中文摘要

我们提出 Athena，一个可扩展的解析式框架，用于完全避免仿真的流片前侧信道泄漏评估。Athena 将静态时序信息与信号概率传播相结合，以建模与时间相关的电路行为，捕捉由输入到达偏斜、毛刺及其他时序效应引起的泄漏。其产生的细粒度泄漏估计可精确识别脆弱信号与时间区间，并支持评估掩码、门尺寸调整和差分逻辑等多种防护措施。我们在 S-Box 与完整密码算法上针对大量防护措施评估 Athena，并在正确性和性能两方面与最先进工具进行比较。在所有基准测试中，Athena 相较现有方法实现超过 400 倍的加速。
