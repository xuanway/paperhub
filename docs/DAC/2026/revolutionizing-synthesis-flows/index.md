## Session Description

Emerging technologies, such as Large Language Models (LLMs), Reinforcement Learning (RL), and Superconducting Circuits, are reshaping hardware synthesis workflows. This session explores the cutting edge of Logic Synthesis and Physical Design, focused on three core themes: 1) AI and Machine Learning in EDA, 2) Advanced Optimization Techniques, and 3) Emerging and Specialized Hardware.
