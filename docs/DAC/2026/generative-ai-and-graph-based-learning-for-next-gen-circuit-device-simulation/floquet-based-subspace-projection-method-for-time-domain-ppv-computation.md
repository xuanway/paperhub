Floquet-Based Subspace Projection Method for Time-Domain PPV Computation

## 中文标题

用于时域 PPV 计算的基于 Floquet 的子空间投影方法

## 英文摘要

Accurate computation of the Perturbation Projection Vector (PPV) is essential for analyzing oscillator phase noise. The PPV corresponds to the steady-state solution of the adjoint system in small-signal oscillator analysis, but in large time-constant oscillators, it is often obscured by numerous slow-decaying modes. To address this, we propose a Floquet-based subspace projection (FSP) method, which confines PPV computation to a low-dimensional subspace spanned by the PPV and slow-decaying solutions. Utilizing the biorthogonality condition, we construct a linear system within this subspace to lock the PPV, since slow-decaying solutions are orthogonal to the large-signal derivative. This linear system is small-scale and can be solved directly, resulting in high-accuracy PPVs. Numerical results demonstrate that FSP significantly enhances both accuracy and computational efficiency.

## 中文摘要

扰动投影向量（PPV）的精确计算对于分析振荡器相位噪声至关重要。在小信号振荡器分析中，PPV 对应于伴随系统的稳态解，但在大时间常数振荡器中，它往往被大量慢衰减模式所掩盖。为解决这一问题，我们提出一种基于 Floquet 的子空间投影（FSP）方法，将 PPV 计算限制在由 PPV 与慢衰减解所张成的低维子空间内。利用双正交条件，我们在该子空间内构造一个线性系统以锁定 PPV，这是因为慢衰减解与大信号导数正交。该线性系统规模很小，可直接求解，从而得到高精度的 PPV。数值结果表明，FSP 显著提升了精度与计算效率。
