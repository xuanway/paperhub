## Session Description

This session explores the transformative intersection of generative AI and graph-based architectures in overcoming the “Complexity Wall” of modern semiconductor design. By integrating in-context learning for circuit simulation, graph transformers for parasitic extraction, and diffusion-based surrogates for TCAD, these works replace traditional, computationally expensive numerical solvers with high-fidelity, scalable models. Additionally, breakthroughs in GPU-accelerated LU factorization and spatio-temporal GNNs demonstrate a paradigm shift toward real-time, structure-adaptive simulation. Together, these papers redefine the efficiency of the analog design loop, offering a roadmap for autonomous, high-performance physical modeling.
