Photomt : Accelerating Zero-Knowledge Proofs with a Photonic-Electronic Merkle Tree Engine
Authors：
[Yan Xu](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=48480677241011544381)，[Mengquan Li](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=58255839977565562111)，[Shu Li](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=07135093551354520821)，[Zhaoyuan Zhang](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=63779565208798825161)，[Kenli Li](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=599143)

## 中文标题

PhotoMT：以光子-电子 Merkle 树引擎加速零知识证明

## 英文摘要

Merkle tree, as a fundamental cryptographic primitive for ensuring user privacy, play a critical role in zero-knowledge proof systems (ZKPs). However, its construction involves numerous computationally intensive Poseidon hashes, creating the primary computational bottleneck in ZKP systems. To address this challenge, we propose PhotoMT, the first photonic-electronic collaborative merkle tree engine. PhotoMT leverages the photonic microring resonator (MR) array for intensive matrix-vector multiplications to boost throughput and energy efficiency, while digital circuits handle control-intensive tasks. Furthermore, PhotoMT incorporates a multi-subtree interleaved execution strategy and an S-BOX bypass computation queue, which together improve hardware utilization and reduce memory overhead. The experiments reveal that PhotoMT boosts throughput by 18.8-20.5× over state-of-the-art AISC-based designs, achieving an energy efficiency gain of 3-5 orders of magnitude against the CPU baseline.

## 中文摘要

Merkle 树作为保障用户隐私的基础密码学原语，在零知识证明系统（ZKP）中扮演着关键角色。然而其构建过程涉及大量计算密集的 Poseidon 哈希，构成 ZKP 系统的主要计算瓶颈。为应对这一挑战，我们提出 PhotoMT，首个光子-电子协同的 Merkle 树引擎。PhotoMT 利用光子微环谐振器（MR）阵列完成密集矩阵-向量乘法以提升吞吐与能效，同时由数字电路处理控制密集型任务。此外，PhotoMT 引入多子树交错执行策略与 S-BOX 旁路计算队列，二者共同提升硬件利用率并降低内存开销。实验表明，PhotoMT 相较最先进的 ASIC 设计吞吐提升 18.8–20.5 倍，相较 CPU 基线实现 3–5 个数量级的能效提升。
