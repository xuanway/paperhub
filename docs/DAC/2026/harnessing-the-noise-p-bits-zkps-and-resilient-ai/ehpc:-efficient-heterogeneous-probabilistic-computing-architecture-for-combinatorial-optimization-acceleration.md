## 中文标题

EHPC：面向组合优化加速的高效异构概率计算架构

## 英文摘要

Combinatorial optimization problem (COP) is known as an NP-hard challenge. Traditional algorithms, including heuristics, analytical methods, and reinforcement learning, are constrained by the Von Neumann architecture, resulting in high computational cost and low efficiency. Probabilistic computing has demonstrated significant potential for efficiently solving COPs. In this work, we propose an efficient heterogeneous probabilistic computing (EHPC) architecture based on volatile RRAM to accelerate combinatorial optimization. We fabricated the OxRAM-multiplexed EHPC circuit and successfully used it to solve the max-cut problem. Furthermore, we implant the max-cut and floorplanning problems into the algorithm of EHPC architecture. In hardware simulations of max-cut problems, EHPC achieves a superior solution quality to existing work under 1 second, using the same computational resources. The results of the floorplanning problems demonstrate that our EHPC architecture significantly boosts computation speed, ranging from 20× to 1500×, with an area expansion <1.7% compared to the best-performing conventional methods, highlighting its advantages in speed, efficiency, and scalability for solving COPs.

## 中文摘要

组合优化问题（COP）被视为 NP 难挑战。传统算法，包括启发式方法、解析方法与强化学习，受限于冯·诺依曼架构，导致计算成本高、效率低。概率计算在高效求解 COP 方面已展现出显著潜力。在本工作中，我们提出一种基于易失性 RRAM 的高效异构概率计算（EHPC）架构，用于加速组合优化。我们制备了 OxRAM 复用的 EHPC 电路，并成功将其用于求解最大割问题。此外，我们把最大割与布局规划问题植入 EHPC 架构的算法之中。在最大割问题的硬件仿真中，EHPC 在相同计算资源下、1 秒内即取得了优于现有工作的解质量。布局规划问题的结果表明，与性能最好的传统方法相比，我们的 EHPC 架构大幅提升了计算速度（20 倍至 1500 倍），而面积仅膨胀不到 1.7%，凸显了其在求解 COP 时在速度、效率与可扩展性方面的优势。
