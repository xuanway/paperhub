## Session Description

This session explores the systems and architectures that enable intelligent machines to operate reliably and efficiently in the real world. We delve into the architectural breakthroughs—from analog accelerators to neuromorphic spiking networks—that allow us to scale complex AI onto the edge. The papers in this session show new approaches to optimize the "brain-to-body" connection of Physical AI, turning massive vision-language models into lean, lightning-fast engines for the real world.
