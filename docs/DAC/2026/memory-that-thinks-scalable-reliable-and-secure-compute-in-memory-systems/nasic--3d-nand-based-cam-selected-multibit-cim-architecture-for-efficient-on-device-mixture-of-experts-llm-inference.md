## 中文标题

NASiC：面向高效端侧混合专家 LLM 推理的基于 3D NAND 的 CAM 选择多位存内计算架构

## 英文摘要

The Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models have emerged as the state-of-the-art paradigm for scaling up large language models (LLMs) without proportionally increased computational cost. However, its on-device deployment faces a critical challenge due to the large memory requirement for storing all expert parameters. 3D NAND-based computing-in-memory (CIM) architectures uniquely offer high storage capacity and reduced data movement, while they are ill-suited for MoE models with dynamically sparse expert activation, leading to a degradation of effective computational parallelism, along with underutilization of multibit storage capability of Flash cells. In this work, we proposed a 3D NAND-based content addressable-selected CIM architecture, dubbed as NASiC , which is tailored to MoE models. By leveraging the intrinsic string structure of 3D NAND technology, NASiC fuses the dynamical expert selection through CAM-based masking mechanism and activated expert computation through CIM into a single computation cycle, eradicating redundant computation and enhancing computational parallelism. Moreover, circuit-level optimizations and multibit CIM cell are co-designed with proposed NASiC architecture, featuring block-wise parallel computation with in-situ signed multibit input and weight expansion, substantially improving the throughput and energy-efficiency of NAND CIM array, as well as the utilization of high-density 3D NAND technology for MoE models. With extensive experimental results, we demonstrate NASiC achieves 4∼114.8× improved performance and 3.9∼70× improved energy efficiency over state-of-the-art designs, along with high accuracy, showing its great potential for efficient on-device MoE LLM inference.

## 中文摘要

混合专家（MoE）模型已成为在计算开销不按比例增长的前提下扩展大语言模型（LLM）规模的前沿范式。然而，其端侧部署因需存储全部专家参数而面临严峻的存储容量挑战。基于 3D NAND 的存内计算（CIM）架构独有地兼具高存储容量与低数据搬运的优势，但其并不适配专家动态稀疏激活的 MoE 模型，导致有效计算并行度下降，且 Flash 单元的多位存储能力未被充分利用。本文提出一种基于 3D NAND 的内容寻址选择 CIM 架构，称为 NASiC，专为 MoE 模型定制。NASiC 利用 3D NAND 技术固有的串（string）结构，将基于 CAM 掩码机制的动态专家选择与基于 CIM 的激活专家计算融合于单个计算周期，从而消除冗余计算并提升计算并行度。此外，电路级优化与多位 CIM 单元同所提 NASiC 架构协同设计，具备分块并行计算以及原地有符号多位输入与权重展开能力，显著提升 NAND CIM 阵列的吞吐率与能效，并提升高密度 3D NAND 技术对 MoE 模型的利用率。大量实验结果表明，NASiC 相较现有最优设计将性能提升 4∼114.8 倍、能效提升 3.9∼70 倍，并保持高精度，展现出其在高效端侧 MoE LLM 推理方面的巨大潜力。
