## 中文标题

S2CIM：一种面向高效可信边缘推理的存内计算安全计算与安全存储架构及其电路—算法协同设计

## 英文摘要

Edge computing that offloads AI inference from the cloud to local devices can significantly reduce interaction latency and improve data privacy, while simultaneously imposing stringent requirements on both efficiency and security. Non-volatile compute-in-memory (nvCIM) boosts efficiency by executing multiply-accumulate (MAC) inside memory arrays, reducing data movement and eliminating weight refresh. However, deploying nvCIM in untrusted environments exposes both user inputs and model weights to multiple security threats: during storage, non-volatility keeps weights vulnerable to physical extraction; during computation, plaintext inputs must be transferred and exposed at runtime. Recent in-memory encryption CIM (IME-CIM) schemes integrate an XOR cipher and in-situ decryption to protect stored weights, yet still require plaintext keys and inputs during inference and often incur up to 2× array area overhead, leaving a security-efficiency gap. This paper proposes S2CIM, a FeFET-based nvCIM architecture with circuit-algorithm co-design to bridge this gap. For security, an Affine-Transform Splitting (ATS) scheme based on randomization strategies protects both offline weights (XORed weights) and online computation (obfuscated inputs) without explicitly exposing plaintext keys or inputs. For efficiency, a Drain-Input Gate-Scan (DIGS) based on 1FeFET-1R achieves low-variation signed multi-bit MAC in minimal cycles with high area efficiency comparable to common nvCIMs. We validate its functionality using an S2CIM macro with 16×16 arrays. Compared with recent IME-CIMs, S2CIM improves area efficiency by up to 16.8× and reduces energy per MAC by up to 6.7×, while ensuring 97.4% encrypted ViT inference accuracy and reducing attack success rates to 50% under all potential threat models.

## 中文摘要

将 AI 推理从云端卸载至本地设备的边缘计算能够显著降低交互延迟并提升数据隐私，但同时对能效与安全性提出了严苛要求。非易失存内计算（nvCIM）通过在存储阵列内部执行乘累加（MAC）运算，减少数据搬运并免除权重刷新，从而提升能效。然而，将 nvCIM 部署于不可信环境会使用户输入与模型权重同时暴露于多种安全威胁：在存储阶段，非易失特性使权重面临被物理提取的风险；在计算阶段，明文输入必须在运行时传输并暴露。近期提出的存内加密 CIM（IME-CIM）方案将 XOR 密码与原地解密相结合以保护所存权重，但推理过程中仍需明文密钥与输入，且往往带来高达 2 倍的阵列面积开销，从而形成安全与能效之间的鸿沟。本文提出 S2CIM，一种基于 FeFET 的 nvCIM 架构，通过电路—算法协同设计弥合该鸿沟。在安全方面，基于随机化策略的仿射变换拆分（ATS）方案同时保护离线权重（经 XOR 的权重）与在线计算（混淆后的输入），而无需显式暴露明文密钥或输入。在能效方面，基于 1FeFET-1R 的漏极输入栅极扫描（DIGS）方案以最少周期实现低变异的有符号多位 MAC，并具备与常规 nvCIM 相当的高面积效率。我们采用包含 16×16 阵列的 S2CIM 宏单元验证了其功能。与近期的 IME-CIM 相比，S2CIM 将面积效率最高提升 16.8 倍，将每次 MAC 能耗最高降低 6.7 倍，同时保证 97.4% 的加密 ViT 推理精度，并在所有潜在威胁模型下将攻击成功率降至 50%。
