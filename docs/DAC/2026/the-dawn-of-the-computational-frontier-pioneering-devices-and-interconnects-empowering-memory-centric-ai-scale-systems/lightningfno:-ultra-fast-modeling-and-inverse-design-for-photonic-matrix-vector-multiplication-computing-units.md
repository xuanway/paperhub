Lightningfno: Ultra-Fast Modeling and Inverse Design for Photonic Matrix-Vector Multiplication Computing Units

## 中文标题

LightningFNO：面向光子矩阵-向量乘法计算单元的超快建模与逆向设计

## 英文摘要

Metasurface-based photonic computing delivers ultra-high computing power density and enables ultrafast, low-power MVM. However, inverse design and system-level simulation remain prohibitively expensive due to large-scale Maxwell solvers. We present LightningFNO, a dual Fourier Neural Operator (FNO) framework that unifies inverse design and fast forward modeling for photonic MVM units. Within this framework, an inverse FNO generates candidate topologies, and a convolutional FNO subsequently predicts their optical responses, thereby facilitating deployment at the neural network level. LightningFNO achieved a speedup of 1,245,550 times compared to adjoint-based optimization for 4×4 designs while maintaining comparable accuracy. Furthermore, fabricated 3×3 prototypes demonstrated a root mean square error (RMSE) of 0.043, and the system attained 97.69% accuracy on the MNIST dataset using metasurface-deployed kernels.

## 中文摘要

基于超表面的光子计算可提供超高的计算功率密度，实现超快、低功耗的 MVM。然而，由于大规模 Maxwell 求解器代价高昂，逆向设计与系统级仿真仍然极为昂贵。我们提出 LightningFNO，一种双傅里叶神经算子（FNO）框架，将光子 MVM 单元的逆向设计与快速前向建模统一起来。在该框架内，逆向 FNO 生成候选拓扑，卷积 FNO 随后预测其光学响应，从而便于在神经网络层面进行部署。对于 4×4 设计，LightningFNO 相比基于伴随法的优化取得 1,245,550 倍的加速，同时保持相当的精度。此外，所制作的 3×3 原型实现了 0.043 的均方根误差（RMSE），系统在使用超表面部署卷积核的情况下于 MNIST 数据集上达到 97.69% 的精度。
