## Session Description

This session highlights how AI is transforming the foundations of computing. Covering intelligent design automation, manufacturing analytics, architecture-aware learning, domain-specific acceleration, and quantum system optimization. The presented works illustrate how artificial intelligence is reshaping computing platforms from silicon technologies to emerging quantum machines.
