Hyperanalog: Domain-Aware Hypergraph Transformer for Analog Circuit Representation Learning
Authors：
[Taejin Paik](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=59925279762991788541)，[Suwan Kim](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=0008808528174359311)

## 中文标题

HyperAnalog：面向模拟电路表征学习的领域感知超图 Transformer

## 英文摘要

Analog circuits exhibit rich multi-terminal relationships that are poorly captured by conventional graph-based learning methods. We introduce HyperAnalog, a domain-aware foundation model that operates directly on a hypergraph representation tailored to transistor-level behavior. HyperAnalog integrates specialized transistor modeling, voltage-aware positional encoding derived from topological distances to VDD/VSS, and a transformer-enhanced hypergraph neural network to improve expressivity and structural discrimination. We evaluate HyperAnalog on circuit classification and specification regression. Experimental results show that HyperAnalog significantly outperforms both graph-and hypergraph-based baselines, proving the efficacy of our domain-aware approach.

## 中文摘要

模拟电路具有丰富的多端关系，传统基于图的学习方法难以充分刻画。我们提出 HyperAnalog，一种领域感知的基础模型，直接作用于针对晶体管级行为定制的超图表征。HyperAnalog 集成了专门的晶体管建模、基于到 VDD/VSS 拓扑距离的电压感知位置编码，以及 Transformer 增强的超图神经网络，从而提升表达能力与结构区分度。我们在电路分类与规格回归任务上评估 HyperAnalog。实验结果表明，HyperAnalog 显著优于基于图与基于超图的基线方法，验证了领域感知方法的有效性。
