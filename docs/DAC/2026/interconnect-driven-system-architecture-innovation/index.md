## Session Description

Emerging interconnect technologies are enabling a new generation of system architectures that scale beyond the limits of conventional monolithic integration. This session highlights recent advances in wafer-scale systems, chiplet-based architectures, and interconnect-aware design space exploration, where novel communication fabrics play a central role in achieving scalability, performance, and energy efficiency.
