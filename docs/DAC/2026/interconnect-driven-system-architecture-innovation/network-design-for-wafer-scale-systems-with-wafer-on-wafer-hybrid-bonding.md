Network Design for Wafer-Scale Systems with Wafer-on-Wafer Hybrid Bonding
Authors：
[Patrick Iff](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=2775153171412309269)，[Tommaso Bonato](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=8179859811858319484)，[Maciej Besta](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=46637680037023349521)，[Luca Benini](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=062443)，[Torsten Hoefler](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=32144523776095363961)

## 中文标题

基于晶圆对晶圆混合键合的晶圆级系统网络设计

## 英文摘要

Transformer-based large language models are increasingly constrained by data movement as communication bandwidth drops sharply beyond the chip boundary. Wafer-scale integration using wafer-on-wafer hybrid bonding alleviates this limitation by providing ultra-high bandwidth between reticles on bonded wafers. In this paper, we investigate how the physical placement of reticles on wafers influences the achievable network topology and the resulting communication performance. Starting from a 2D mesh-like baseline, we propose four reticle placements (Aligned, Interleaved, Rotated, and Contoured) that improve throughput by up to 250%, reduce latency by up to 36%, and decrease energy per transmitted byte by up to 38%.

## 中文摘要

随着通信带宽在跨越芯片边界后急剧下降，基于 Transformer 的大语言模型日益受到数据搬运的制约。采用晶圆对晶圆混合键合的晶圆级集成通过在键合晶圆上的各掩模版之间提供超高带宽，缓解了这一限制。本文研究晶圆上掩模版的物理布局如何影响可实现的网络拓扑及由此产生的通信性能。从二维类网格（2D mesh）基线出发，我们提出四种掩模版布局方案（对齐式、交错式、旋转式和轮廓式），可将吞吐率最多提升 250%、时延最多降低 36%、每传输字节能耗最多降低 38%。
