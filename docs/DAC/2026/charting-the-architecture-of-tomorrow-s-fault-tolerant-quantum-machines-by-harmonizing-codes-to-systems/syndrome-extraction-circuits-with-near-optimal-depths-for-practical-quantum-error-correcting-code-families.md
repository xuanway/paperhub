## 中文标题

面向实用量子纠错码族的近最优深度伴随式提取电路

## 英文摘要

Fault-tolerant quantum computing relies on low-depth and high-fidelity syndrome extraction for quantum error correcting codes. We propose a scheduling strategy for syndrome extraction circuits for the broad class of quasi-abelian lifted-product codes, encompassing both hypergraph product and bicycle codes. Our approach constructs syndrome extraction circuits with CNOT depths no more than one layer above the fundamental lower bound, frequently achieving optimal depth. A pipelined variant further reduces the average depth per round, and the strategy generalizes naturally to higher-dimensional product codes. This scheduling framework enables computational speedups for quantum computing architectures built on these codes, all while preserving high-fidelity error correction.

## 中文摘要

容错量子计算依赖于量子纠错码的低深度、高保真度伴随式提取。我们针对广泛的准阿贝尔提升乘积码类（涵盖超图乘积码和自行车码）提出一种伴随式提取电路的调度策略。我们的方法所构造的伴随式提取电路，其 CNOT 深度不超过基本下界之上的一层，且常常达到最优深度。一种流水线变体进一步降低了每轮的平均深度，并且该策略自然推广到高维乘积码。这一调度框架使基于这些码构建的量子计算架构获得计算加速，同时保持高保真度的纠错。
