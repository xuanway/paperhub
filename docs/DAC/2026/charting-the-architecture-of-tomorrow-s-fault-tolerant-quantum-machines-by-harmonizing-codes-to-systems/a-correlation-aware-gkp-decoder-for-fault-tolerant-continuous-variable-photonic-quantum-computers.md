A Correlation-Aware GKP Decoder for Fault-Tolerant Continuous-Variable Photonic Quantum Computers
Authors：
[Ryosuke Matsuo](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=626843)，[Junichiro Kadomoto](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=5318828266685005483)

## 中文标题

面向容错连续变量光子量子计算机的相关性感知 GKP 解码器

## 英文摘要

The Gottesman–Kitaev–Preskill (GKP) encoding is a promising approach for realizing fault-tolerant continuous-variable (CV) photonic quantum computers. Recent studies have investigated various GKP decoding algorithms, among which correlation-aware methods that exploit inter-qubit correlations achieve significantly improved error-correction performance. However, hardware implementation of GKP decoding remains largely unexplored. Error decoding in CV photonic quantum computers must operate within tens of nanoseconds to match the optical clock frequency. A straightforward hardware implementation of correlation-aware decoding introduces substantial computational latency due to its arithmetic complexity. To address this challenge, this paper proposes a high-accuracy and low-latency accelerator architecture optimized for correlation-aware GKP decoding. Logic synthesis using 7-nm FinFET technology demonstrates that our decoder can complete correlation-aware GKP decoding within 12.96 ns. In addition, comprehensive design space exploration is conducted to evaluate tradeoffs among decoding accuracy, latency, and circuit area, leading to design guidelines for future correlation-aware GKP decoder implementations.

## 中文摘要

Gottesman–Kitaev–Preskill（GKP）编码是实现容错连续变量（CV）光子量子计算机的一种颇具前景的途径。近期研究已探索了多种 GKP 解码算法，其中利用量子比特间相关性的相关性感知方法取得了显著改善的纠错性能。然而，GKP 解码的硬件实现仍鲜有探索。连续变量光子量子计算机中的错误解码必须在数十纳秒内完成，以匹配光学时钟频率。由于算术复杂度较高，直接对相关性感知解码进行硬件实现会引入可观的计算延迟。为应对这一挑战，本文提出一种专为相关性感知 GKP 解码优化的高精度、低延迟加速器架构。采用 7 nm FinFET 工艺进行的逻辑综合表明，我们的解码器可在 12.96 ns 内完成相关性感知 GKP 解码。此外，我们开展了全面的设计空间探索，以评估解码精度、延迟与电路面积之间的权衡，从而为未来相关性感知 GKP 解码器的实现提供设计指导。
