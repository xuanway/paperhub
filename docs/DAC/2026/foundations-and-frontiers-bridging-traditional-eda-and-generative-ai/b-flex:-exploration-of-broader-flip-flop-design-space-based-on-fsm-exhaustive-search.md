B-Flex: Exploration of Broader Flip-Flop Design Space Based on FSM Exhaustive Search
Authors：
[Hyunsung Jeong](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=87734473585678220371)，[Kyounghun Kang](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=08325972257108635381)，[Wanyeong Jung](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=04210385845469915071)，[Jongeun Lee](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=25253050416479025401)

## 中文标题

B-Flex：基于 FSM 穷举搜索的触发器设计空间拓展探索

## 英文摘要

Flip-flops (FFs) are a critical component affecting system-level power, performance, and area (PPA). Many logic-based FF design methods have been introduced to expand the scope of topology exploration beyond intuition. However, their exploration scope is still limited to 2-bit finite state machines (FSMs) because of inefficient search space representation. We present B-Flex, an automated FF FSM search that integrates a complete, graph-based behavioral equivalence check into a pruning-based generative search. This efficient and scalable approach vastly expands the design space and allows exhaustive exploration of 3-bit-state FSMs. B-Flex has identified over 568 million valid FF mechanisms, including many novel 3-bit-state FF designs. Topology synthesis on 20 sampled FSMs has produced several high-performance FF circuits that outperform conventional FFs. For instance, FF1, achieves a 2.53× speedup over a transmission-gate FF (TGFF) and improved metastability window at 0.9 V.

## 中文摘要

触发器（FF）是影响系统级功耗、性能与面积（PPA）的关键部件。为将拓扑探索的范围拓展到直觉之外，已有许多基于逻辑的 FF 设计方法被提出。然而，由于搜索空间表示效率低下，其探索范围仍局限于 2 位有限状态机（FSM）。我们提出 B-Flex，一种自动化的 FF 有限状态机搜索方法，将完整的、基于图的行为等价性检查集成到基于剪枝的生成式搜索中。这种高效且可扩展的方法极大扩展了设计空间，支持对 3 位状态 FSM 的穷举探索。B-Flex 已识别出超过 5.68 亿种有效 FF 机制，其中包含许多新颖的 3 位状态 FF 设计。对 20 个采样 FSM 进行拓扑综合，产出了若干性能优于传统 FF 的高性能 FF 电路。例如，FF1 相较传输门触发器（TGFF）实现 2.53 倍加速，并在 0.9 V 下具有更优的亚稳态窗口。
