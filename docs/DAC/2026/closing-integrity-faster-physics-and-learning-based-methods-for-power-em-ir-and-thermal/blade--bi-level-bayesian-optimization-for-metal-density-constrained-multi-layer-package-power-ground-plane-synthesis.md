## 中文标题

BLADE：面向金属密度约束的多层封装电源/地平面综合的双层贝叶斯优化

## 英文摘要

In advanced packages, large power/ground (P/G) planes are essential for power integrity but prone to warpage. While a uniform metal-density constraint combats warpage, mandatory degassing holes complicate adherence to it and degrade IR-drop. We propose BLADE, the first P/G plane synthesis methodology that enforces this constraint while optimizing power integrity. BLADE expands P/G planes from skeletons under metal density control to ensure connectivity, naturally leaving holes for degassing. Guided by a fast IR-drop evaluator, our bi-level Bayesian optimization framework efficiently navigates the design space. Experiments on six SiP cases demonstrate that BLADE simultaneously satisfies the metal-density constraint and achieves superior IR-drop performance.

## 中文摘要

在先进封装中，大面积电源/地（P/G）平面对于电源完整性不可或缺，但容易发生翘曲。均匀的金属密度约束可抑制翘曲，然而强制性的排气孔使其难以满足，并会恶化 IR Drop。我们提出 BLADE，这是首个在满足该约束的同时优化电源完整性的 P/G 平面综合方法。BLADE 在金属密度控制下从骨架扩展 P/G 平面以确保连通性，并自然地留出排气孔。在快速 IR Drop 评估器的引导下，我们的双层贝叶斯优化框架能够高效地搜索设计空间。在六个 SiP 案例上的实验表明，BLADE 同时满足了金属密度约束并取得了更优的 IR Drop 性能。
