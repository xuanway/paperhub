EastPCG: An Efficient and Self-Tuning Graph Sparsification Based PCG Solver for Circuit Simulation
Authors：
[Baiyu Chen](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=4148599812212512362)，[Wenjian Yu](https://63dac.conference-program.com/?post_type=page&p=15&uid=980243)

## 中文标题

EastPCG：面向电路仿真的高效自调优图稀疏化 PCG 求解器

## 英文摘要

Graph spectral sparsification plays a crucial role in numerous EDA applications and is a promising preconditioning technique for preconditioned conjugate gradient (PCG) solvers in both theory and practice. In this paper, a highly efficient and stable graph sparsification algorithm based on spectral probability is proposed. Meanwhile, targeting a minimum total solution time of the linear equation with multiple right-hand sides, an efficient self-tuning PCG framework powered by neural networks is proposed. By combining the proposed techniques, an efficient and self-tuning graph sparsification-based PCG solver, named EastPCG, is developed. Extensive experiments on various benchmarks have demonstrated the advantages of the proposed algorithms over existing counterparts.

## 中文摘要

图谱稀疏化在众多 EDA 应用中起着至关重要的作用，无论在理论上还是实践中，都是一种面向预处理共轭梯度（PCG）求解器颇具前景的预处理技术。本文提出了一种基于谱概率的高效且稳定的图稀疏化算法。同时，针对具有多个右端项的线性方程组总求解时间最小化问题，提出了一种由神经网络驱动的高效自调优 PCG 框架。通过结合上述技术，开发了一个高效且自调优的、基于图稀疏化的 PCG 求解器，命名为 EastPCG。在多种基准测试上的大量实验证明了所提算法相对于现有同类算法的优势。
