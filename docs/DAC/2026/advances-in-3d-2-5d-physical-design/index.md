## Session Description

Collectively, this session highlights how design automation is evolving to unlock the full potential of 3D and 2.5D integration. By addressing the intertwined challenges of timing, thermal constraints, and physical implementation, these innovations pave the way for scalable, high-performance, and energy-efficient multi-die systems.
