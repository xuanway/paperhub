## 中文标题

LLMVA：LLM 赋能的 Verilog-A 迭代式 MRAM 设计工艺协同优化

## 英文摘要

Emerging device characteristics modeling is indispensable for potential circuit design integration. In the modeling process, the Verilog-A language is employed, leveraging the physical parameters and test results of the device. This work demonstrates the large language model (LLM) empowered Verilog-A iterative (LLMVA) for the modeling process of spin-transfer-torque magnetic-tunnel-junction (STT-MTJ). LLM enhances the quick interaction with device-level characteristics and circuit-level indicators, and realize design technology co-optimization (DTCO). To the best of the authors’ knowledge, this is the first work to use LLM for device modeling and MRAM DTCO. The iterative test results show that the deviation between Front-test/Post-test results of MTJ does not exceed 9.2%, proving the effectiveness of the proposed LLMVA modeling process. With the proposed LLMVA agent, we have decreased our designer and time cost by about 50% in MRAM tape-out process.

## 中文摘要

新兴器件特性建模对于潜在的电路设计集成不可或缺。在建模过程中，采用 Verilog-A 语言，并利用器件的物理参数与测试结果。本工作展示了由大语言模型（LLM）赋能的 Verilog-A 迭代（LLMVA）方法用于自旋转移矩磁隧道结（STT-MTJ）的建模过程。LLM 增强了对器件级特性与电路级指标的快速交互，并实现了设计工艺协同优化（DTCO）。据作者所知，这是首个利用 LLM 进行器件建模与 MRAM DTCO 的工作。迭代测试结果表明，MTJ 的前测/后测结果偏差不超过 9.2%，证明了所提 LLMVA 建模流程的有效性。借助所提 LLMVA 智能体，我们在 MRAM 流片过程中将设计人员与时间成本降低了约 50%。
