# Prometheus: Toward Resilient Data Centers through Optimized Cooling Infrastructure

**中文标题：** Prometheus: 通过优化制冷基础设施迈向高韧性数据中心

**所属 Session：** [Session 5B](index.md) · [ISCA 2026](../index.md)

**作者：** Sourav Patel, Pratyush Kumar, Youzhi Liang, Thomas Kowalski, Nick Care, Wenjie Dong, Greg Imwalle, Rita Lu, Jeremy Rice, Nick Saddock, Rick Vengalath, Urs Hölzle, Benjamin C. Lee, Parthasarathy Ranganathan

**来源：** ISCA 2026 · [官方日程](https://www.iscaconf.org/isca2026/program/)

## 英文摘要

Data center design assumptions are increasingly at risk from severe weather events. The framework Prometheus forecasts future temperatures and guides infrastructure design on multiple timescales. Using machine learning on historical and simulated climate data, Prometheus indicates that data centers must increase cooling capacity by 11% on average and up to 48% at the sites most challenged. 30% of data centers lack sufficient load flexibility and 12% already face a high probability of exceeding their design temperatures and require upgrades.

## 中文摘要

数据中心的设计假设正日益受到极端天气事件的威胁。Prometheus 这一框架可预测未来温度，并在多个时间尺度上指导基础设施设计。通过对历史与模拟气候数据应用机器学习，Prometheus 指出数据中心必须平均将制冷能力提升 11%，而在挑战最严峻的站点需提升高达 48%。30% 的数据中心缺乏足够的负载灵活性，12% 的数据中心已面临超出其设计温度的高概率风险，需要进行升级改造。
