# SATIC: An Optimizing Ising Compiler for SAT(isfiability)

**中文标题：** SATIC: 面向 SAT（可满足性）的优化 Ising 编译器

**所属 Session：** [Session 9C](index.md) · [ISCA 2026](../index.md)

**作者：** Ahmet Efe, Hüsrev Cılasun, Abhimanyu Kumar, Nafisa S. Prova, Ziqing Zeng, Tahmida Islam, Ruihong Yin, Chaohui Li, Peter Kreye, Chris H. Kim, Sachin S. Sapatnekar, Ulya R. Karpuzcu

**来源：** ISCA 2026 · [官方日程](https://www.iscaconf.org/isca2026/program/)

## 英文摘要

Ising machines show great potential as hardware solvers for combinatorial optimization problems such as Boolean Satisfiability (SAT) – one of the hardest classic problems of practical importance. Unlocking this potential, however, is only possible by bridging the gap between SAT problem characteristics and Ising machine specifics. In this paper, we introduce a novel optimizing compiler featuring a bag of powerful heuristic tricks, SATIC, toward this goal. We evaluate SATIC on a representative manufactured Ising chip. Our measurements show that SATIC enables Ising machines to solve up to 73× larger SAT problems than the hardware capacity would otherwise allow, demonstrating unmatched performance and scalability.

## 中文摘要

Ising 机作为组合优化问题（如布尔可满足性（SAT）——最具实际重要性的最困难经典问题之一）的硬件求解器，展现出巨大潜力。然而，只有弥合 SAT 问题特征与 Ising 机特性之间的差距，才能释放这一潜力。在本文中，我们为此目标引入了一种新颖的优化编译器 SATIC，它配备了一整套强大的启发式技巧。我们在一个具有代表性的已制造 Ising 芯片上评估了 SATIC。我们的测量表明，SATIC 使 Ising 机能够求解比硬件容量原本允许的大至 73 倍的 SAT 问题，展示了无与伦比的性能和可扩展性。
