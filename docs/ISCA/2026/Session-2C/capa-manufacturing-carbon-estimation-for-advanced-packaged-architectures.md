# CAPA: Manufacturing Carbon Estimation for Advanced-Packaged Architectures

**中文标题：** CAPA: 面向先进封装架构的制造碳排放估算

**所属 Session：** [Session 2C](index.md) · [ISCA 2026](../index.md)

**作者：** Jingyang Liu, Gwenith Bowker-Bafna, Yuke Zhang, Natalie Enright Jerger

**来源：** ISCA 2026 · [官方日程](https://www.iscaconf.org/isca2026/program/)

## 英文摘要

To meet growing compute demand, hyperscalers are rapidly deploying new data centre hardware, which embodies significant carbon. This aggressive growth of compute infrastructure jeopardizes their carbon reduction goals. While existing carbon tools estimate the manufacturing carbon footprint of integrated circuits (ICs), they do not reasonably model the high-performance advanced-packaged processors that dominate data centres and supercomputers. To bridge this gap, we propose a tool called CAPA, which models manufacturing Carbon for Advanced-Packaged Architectures. CAPA incorporates a binning yield model, high-bandwidth memory (HBM) carbon estimates, and support for complex architectures that use a mixture of integration techniques, such as 3.5D. By using CAPA to study widely used high-performance processors, we reveal opportunities for carbon savings through systematic binning and testing strategies, and we highlight HBM as a major carbon contributor. CAPA provides insights about the carbon footprint of cutting-edge architectures, opening new avenues for mitigating the environmental impact of the ever-growing computing industry.

## 中文摘要

为满足日益增长的计算需求，超大规模云服务商正在快速部署新的数据中心硬件，这些硬件蕴含大量碳排放。这种激进的算力基础设施建设危及了它们的碳减排目标。虽然现有的碳足迹工具能够估算集成电路（IC）的制造碳排放，但它们未能合理建模占据数据中心和超级计算机主导地位的高性能先进封装处理器。为弥补这一空白，我们提出了CAPA工具，用于建模先进封装架构的制造碳排放。CAPA集成了分档良率模型、高带宽存储器（HBM）碳排放估算，并支持使用混合集成技术（如3.5D）的复杂架构。通过使用CAPA研究广泛使用的高性能处理器，我们揭示了通过系统化分档和测试策略实现碳减排的机会，并指出HBM是主要的碳排放贡献者。CAPA提供了关于前沿架构碳足迹的洞察，为减轻不断增长的计算行业对环境的影响开辟了新途径。
