# TierX: A Simulation Framework for Multi-tier BCI System Design Evaluation and Exploration

**中文标题：** TierX：面向多层次 BCI 系统设计评估与探索的仿真框架

**所属 Session：** [Session 9C](index.md) · [ASPLOS 2026](../index.md)

**作者：** Seunghyun Song (Seoul National University), Yeongwoo Jang (Seoul National University), Daye Jung (Seoul National University), Kyungsoo Park (Seoul National University), Donghan Kim (Seoul National University), Gwangjin Kim (Seoul National University), Hunjun Lee (Hanyang University), Jerald Yoo (Seoul National University), Jangwoo Kim (Seoul National University / MangoBoost)

**单位：** Seoul National University；Hanyang University；Seoul National University / MangoBoost

## 英文摘要

Brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) have made remarkable progress in recent years, driven by advances in neuroscience and clinical applications. For practical use, underlying processing systems must meet strict latency and power budgets. However, existing BCI systems typically rely on a single processing node to handle the entire workload, making it difficult to satisfy these budgets across diverse applications. In this work, we present TierX, the first simulation framework for design space exploration of multi-tier BCI systems. TierX models heterogeneous processing nodes across tiers, including implanted processors, body-attached devices, and external servers, together with diverse communication and powering methods. It navigates the extensive design space to identify optimal (1) workload partitioning options and (2) system configurations that leverage the strengths of each tier. We validate TierX on representative system configurations and demonstrate its effectiveness across diverse use cases.

## 中文摘要

近年来，在神经科学与临床应用的推动下，脑机接口（BCI）取得了显著进展。为了满足实际使用需求，底层处理系统必须满足严格的延迟和功耗预算。然而，现有 BCI 系统通常依赖单一处理节点来处理整个工作负载，难以在各种不同的应用中满足这些预算。在本工作中，我们提出 TierX，这是首个用于多层次 BCI 系统设计空间探索的仿真框架。TierX 对跨层次的异构处理节点建模，包括植入式处理器、贴身设备以及外部服务器，并涵盖多种通信与供电方式。它遍历广阔的设计空间，以确定最优的 (1) 工作负载划分选项和 (2) 系统配置，从而发挥每个层次的优势。我们在具有代表性的系统配置上验证了 TierX，并展示了其在各种不同的用例中的有效性。
