# QTurbo: A Robust and Efficient Compiler for Analog Quantum Simulation

**中文标题：** QTurbo：面向模拟量子模拟的鲁棒且高效的编译器

**所属 Session：** [Session 1C](index.md) · [ASPLOS 2026](../index.md)

**作者：** Junyu Zhou (University of Pennsylvania), Yuhao Liu (University of Pennsylvania), Shize Che (University of Pennsylvania), Anupam Mitra (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory), Efekan Kökcü (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory), Ermal Rrapaj (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory), Costin Iancu (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory), Gushu Li (University of Pennsylvania)

**来源：** ASPLOS 2026 · Volume 1 · [论文 DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3760250.3762227) · [官方日程](https://www.asplos-conference.org/asplos2026/program/index.html)

## 英文摘要

Analog quantum simulation leverages native hardware dynamics to emulate complex quantum systems with great efficiency by bypassing the quantum circuit abstraction. However, conventional compilation methods for analog simulators are typically labor-intensive, prone to errors, and computationally demanding. This paper introduces QTurbo, a powerful analog quantum simulation compiler designed to significantly enhance compilation efficiency and optimize hardware execution time. By generating precise and noise-resilient pulse schedules, our approach ensures greater accuracy and reliability, outperforming the existing state-of-theart approach.

## 中文摘要

模拟量子模拟绕开量子电路抽象，利用硬件原生动力学以极高的效率模拟复杂量子系统。然而，针对模拟仿真器的传统编译方法通常劳动密集、易于出错且计算代价高昂。本文介绍 QTurbo，一个强大的模拟量子模拟编译器，旨在显著提升编译效率并优化硬件执行时间。通过生成精确且抗噪声的脉冲调度，我们的方法确保了更高的准确性与可靠性，超越了现有的最先进方法。
