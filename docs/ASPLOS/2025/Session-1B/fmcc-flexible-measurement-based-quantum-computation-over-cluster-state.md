# FMCC: Flexible Measurement-based Quantum Computation over Cluster State

**中文标题：** FMCC：面向簇态的灵活测量型量子计算

**所属 Session：** [Session 1B](index.md) · [ASPLOS 2025](../index.md)

**作者：** Yingheng Li (University of Pittsburgh), Aditya Pawar (University of Pittsburgh), Zewei Mo (University of Pittsburgh), Youtao Zhang (University of Pittsburgh), Jun Yang (University of Pittsburgh), Xulong Tang (University of Pittsburgh)

**单位：** University of Pittsburgh

**来源：** ASPLOS 2025 · [论文 DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3622781.3674185) · [官方日程](https://www.asplos-conference.org/asplos2025/program.html)

## 英文摘要

Measurement-based quantum computing (MBQC) is a promising quantum computing paradigm that performs computation through “one-way” measurements on entangled quantum qubits. It is widely used in photonic quantum computing (PQC), where the computation is carried out on photonic cluster states (i.e., a 2-D mesh of entangled photons). In MBQC-based PQC, the cluster state depth (i.e., the length of one-way measurements) plays an important role in the overall execution time and circuit error. In this paper, we propose FMCC, a compilation framework that employs dynamic programming with heuristics to efficiently minimize the cluster state depth. Experimental results on six quantum applications show that FMCC achieves 51.7%, 57.4%, and 56.8% average depth reductions in small, medium, and large qubit counts compared to the state-of-the-art MBQC compilations.

## 中文摘要

测量型量子计算（MBQC）是一种颇具前景的量子计算范式，它通过对纠缠的量子比特执行“单向”测量来完成计算。它被广泛应用于光子量子计算（PQC）中，这类计算在光子簇态（即纠缠光子构成的二维网格）上执行。在基于 MBQC 的 PQC 中，簇态深度（即单向测量的长度）对整体执行时间与线路误差有重要影响。本文提出 FMCC，一个采用动态规划并结合启发式方法的编译框架，能够高效地最小化簇态深度。在六个量子应用上的实验结果表明，与最先进的 MBQC 编译方法相比，FMCC 在小、中、大量子比特规模下分别实现了 51.7%、57.4% 和 56.8% 的平均深度降低。
